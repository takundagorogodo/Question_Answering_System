
GPT means Generative Pre-trained Transformer.
GPT-style models are transformer-based language models.
GPT models are primarily decoder-based architectures.
GPT models commonly use autoregressive generation.
An autoregressive model predicts the next token.
Next-token prediction is a central GPT training objective.

Causal masking prevents access to future target tokens.

Higher temperature can increase variation.

Top-p sampling selects tokens from a probability mass.
